Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 28, 2026  ·  Last updated: April 28, 2026

EtsyTrueProfit ("we", "our", or "us") operates the EtsyTrue — Etsy Profit Calculator Chrome Extension (the "Extension"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have over your data.

By installing or using the Extension you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you have any questions, email us at vishala4716@gmail.com.

Short version: Your Etsy data and cost inputs never leave your device on the free tier. We only receive your email address if you upgrade to Pro.

1. What Data We Collect

Etsy OAuth Access Token

When you connect your Etsy shop, Etsy's official OAuth 2.0 flow issues an access token to the Extension. This token is stored exclusively in chrome.storage.sync — it never leaves your browser except to make read-only API calls directly to Etsy's servers. We do not store it on any server we operate.

Etsy Shop Order and Listing Data

The Extension fetches your Etsy orders and listings via the Etsy API to calculate profit figures. This data is processed locally in your browser and displayed inside the Extension. On the free tier it is never transmitted to our servers. It is held in memory only for the duration of your session and is not persisted between sessions.

Material Costs and Labor Hours You Enter

When you type in material costs per unit or minutes to make a product, those values are saved in chrome.storage.local — local to your device and browser profile. They are never transmitted anywhere.

Email Address (Pro subscribers only)

If you upgrade to Pro, you provide an email address at checkout. We receive that email from our payment processor (Lemon Squeezy) and store it in our subscription database (Supabase) solely to verify your Pro status when the Extension checks for an active subscription. We do not use it for marketing without your consent.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your Etsy password. We use OAuth — your password goes only to Etsy, never to us.
  • Your payment card details. All payment processing is handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy. We never see your card number, CVV, or billing address.
  • Your browsing history. The Extension only activates on Etsy-related pages and our own dashboard.
  • Data from non-Etsy websites. We have no access to any pages outside of the Etsy domain and our Extension's own UI.
  • Aggregate analytics or telemetry. We do not send usage events, crash reports, or any analytics to third-party tracking services.

3. How Data Is Stored

  • OAuth token: chrome.storage.sync — encrypted by Chrome and optionally synced to your signed-in Google account across devices. Governed by Google's security practices.
  • Cost data you enter: chrome.storage.local — stays on your device. Never synced, never transmitted.
  • Pro subscription record: Supabase database — we store your email address and plan status only. Supabase uses industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Etsy API — the official Etsy developer API, used with read-only permissions to fetch your shop's order and listing data. Governed by Etsy's Privacy Policy.
  • Lemon Squeezy — payment processing for Pro subscriptions. They collect and process your payment details. Governed by Lemon Squeezy's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — database hosting for Pro subscriber records (email + plan status only). Governed by Supabase's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any other third parties.

5. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate with the Etsy API and fetch your shop data for profit calculations.
  • To store your cost inputs so you don't have to re-enter them each session.
  • To verify your Pro subscription status (email only, Pro subscribers).
  • To respond to your support enquiries.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the Extension.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data (OAuth token, cost inputs) is deleted automatically when you uninstall the Extension from Chrome. You can also clear it at any time via Chrome's extension settings or by disconnecting your Etsy account inside the Extension.

Pro subscription records in our Supabase database are retained while your subscription is active and for up to 90 days after cancellation for accounting purposes. To request immediate deletion, email us at vishala4716@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my data" and we will remove your record within 14 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data: OAuth tokens are stored in Chrome's encrypted storage, data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS, and our Supabase database uses row-level security. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at vishala4716@gmail.com.
